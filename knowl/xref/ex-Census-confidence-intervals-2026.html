Example 7.4. US Census Data Confidence Intervals October 2026.

Now, look at US Census data from October 2026 (This data can be found at https://www.census.gov/data/datasets/time-series/demo/cps/cps-basic.html)
(a)
Use the sample data to estimate the average number of people living in a household (among households with a non-zero number of people). (Look at “HRNUMHOU”.)
(b)
Use the sample data to estimate the proportion of households in the US in which someone owns a business or a farm. (Look at “HUBUS”.)
